# AQI Rx · Green Minutes

**Go Out, Clock Green.** Explore air quality and plan your movement in India.

## [Open the app → www.greenminutes.in](https://www.greenminutes.in)

AQI Rx brings air-quality readings, forecasts and on-demand explanations into one web app. Use it on a phone or desktop to explore when and where you want to exercise.

### Explore the app

| View | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | See current India/CPCB AQI, PM₂.₅ and ozone. Move the clock to compare readings at the same hour over the past seven days, then request an explanation. |
| **Planner** | Choose an exercise location and forecast hour, explore the next 36 hours, and preview activities with the green/yellow minute clock. |
| **Progress** | Record completed workouts and follow your minutes, XP and milestones. |
| **News & Research** | Discover recent air-pollution news from India and scientific research from around the world, with links to original sources. |

### Data and explanations

- **Google Maps Air Quality:** current conditions, historical readings and forecasts.
- **Cerebras:** on-demand explanations of the supplied air-quality data, distinguishing history from forecasts.
- **Exa:** searches for news and scientific publications.

Forecasts are estimates, and missing readings remain unavailable. Workout progress is stored in your browser; it is not automatically synced between devices or website addresses.

### Understanding the clock

Green and yellow minutes are **experimental exposure-planning benchmarks and reward rules**. They are not medically validated safe exercise durations, damage thresholds or proof of health benefits. Follow official air-quality guidance independently of the clock's colour. The app explains its assumptions and limitations under **How the clock works** in News & Research.
